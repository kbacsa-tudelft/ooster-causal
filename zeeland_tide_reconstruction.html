Prediction is one-step-ahead (15 min) using the trained fitting network + the final discovered graph, not a multi-step forecast — the model sees the real previous reading at every step. Reading this as a forecast would understate the error; it answers "does the learned graph explain tomorrow's reading given today's," which is what CUTS+ optimizes.

Reconstructed from cuts_plus_fitting_model.pt + cuts_plus_graph.npy (convergence_sweep_zeeland/sparsity_fine_08), replayed on the same train/val/score session split used at training time. sparsity_fine_08 was the strongest of 13 configs swept on Zeeland for held-out val MSE (see compare_fill_policies.py).
