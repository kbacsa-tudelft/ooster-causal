Zeeland storm surge, Dec 2013 — interpolated flood-risk map (5-min model)

A continuous risk surface over the whole visible area: each gauge contributes a soft gaussian impulse, so the surface fades to nothing away from every gauge instead of extrapolating - blue is calm, red is at or above each gauge's own POT/EVT flood threshold (risk = |observed − predicted| / threshold, so every gauge's very different natural variability is normalized onto the same 0-1+ scale). Each gauge's contribution lingers and decays with a 3-hour half-life rather than switching on and off hour to hour, so a real spike fades out gradually instead of spiking and vanishing. Drag the slider or press play to scrub through the event.

calm  →  at/above flood threshold
Model: zeeland_flood_eval_5min (5-min resample, CUTS+ causal discovery + hold-out 1-step prediction). Risk = max(|observed − predicted| this hour, previous risk × decay) / that gauge's own POT threshold (cm), decay = 0.5^(1/3) per hour (3-hour half-life) - a peak-hold-with-decay filter, computed once at build time from the trained checkpoint's saved residual z-scores and POT/EVT threshold refit at risk=0.001 / initial_level=0.99 / num_candidates=100 (the best-F1 point from sweep_pot_params.py). The surface is a gaussian-kernel interpolation across the 40 gauges (of 77 total) with enough validation data to fit a threshold at all, and is drawn over both land and water - the model only ever had Zeeland's own gauges to work with, so risk away from them is extrapolated, not measured; the surrounding Dutch/Belgian coastline is shown for geographic context only, not scored. Event window (2013-12-06 01:50 → 2013-12-06 04:20) shown as a red band on the slider track. Coastline: OpenStreetMap natural=water bodies for Zeeland's own estuaries (Oosterschelde, Grevelingenmeer, Haringvliet, Veerse Meer, Volkerak and others) plus Natural Earth's open-ocean outline for the wider coast, over a Dutch-municipality land fill (CBS/Kadaster). The Westerschelde's open tidal channel isn't separately water-tagged in OSM and isn't approximated here, so it still reads as land between Vlissingen, Terneuzen, Hansweert and Rilland-Bath.
